游戏规则说明 / Game Rules & Instructions

一、 游戏目标 / Game Objective

将一副打乱的扑克牌（52张）全部按同花色从 A 到 K 的顺序顺连整理，并最终从桌面全部回收消除。桌面所有牌收完即通关。
Arrange and clear a shuffled deck of 52 cards into sequential order by suit from A to K. Clear all cards from the table to win.

二、 牌面布局 / Layout

开局分发至 7 个纵向列。底部为明牌（正面朝上），上方压着的为暗牌（背面朝上）。使用全套黑桃、梅花、红桃、方片。
Cards are dealt into 7 columns. Bottom cards face up; covered cards face down. Uses all 4 suits: Spades, Clubs, Hearts, and Diamonds.

三、 核心移动规则 / Core Movement Rules

  • 单张移动：点数小 1 且同花色的明牌，可移动到大 1 的同花色牌下方。
    Single Card: A card can move onto a card of the same suit that is 1 rank higher.
  • 多张连牌移动：同列中已排好序的同花色连牌组可整体拖动。
    Sequential Groups: Properly sequenced same-suit card groups can be moved together.
  • 空列规则：空列仅允许 K（国王）或以 K 开头的合法连牌组入驻。
    Empty Columns: Only a King (K) or a sequence starting with K can fill an empty column.
  • 翻开暗牌：明牌移走后，其上方的暗牌会自动翻开。
    Revealing Cards: When a face-up card is moved away, the hidden card beneath it automatically flips face up.

四、 自动回收机制 / Auto-Collection

当某列底部凑齐从 A 到 K（同花色）的完整序列时，系统自动回收并消除。
When a complete sequence from A to K of the same suit forms at the bottom of a column, the system automatically collects and clears it.

五、 新手建议 / Beginner Tips

  • 优先移动明牌去解锁并翻开被压住的暗牌。
    Prioritize moving face-up cards to reveal hidden cards underneath.
  • 同花色接龙能保证牌组保持整体可移动性。
    Sticking to same-suit builds keeps your card groups fully movable.
  • 空列非常珍贵，切勿轻易填满。
    Empty columns are extremely valuable; do not fill them up carelessly.
